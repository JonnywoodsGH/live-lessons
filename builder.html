1 · Where will the question go?

For PowerPoint, Keynote, Google Slides or Canva lessons exported as a PDF. You'll get a question file to upload with the PDF on the teacher page.

2 · The question
Fill in 2 to 4 options, tick the right one, and say why each wrong one is wrong. Students see that as "Why not B". The misconception ID (e.g. MC-RAM-01) is optional; it goes in the CSV export so you can track that misconception across lessons.
    3 · Download

    Questions you add appear here. You can add several before downloading.

    Then upload the updated deck on the teacher page as usual (same lesson ID to replace the old one). Your original file isn't changed.

    On the teacher page, choose the PDF and this question file together when adding the lesson.
