Optional · once

    
Email students their notes

    
Students can type short notes during a live lesson (✎ Notes at the top of their screen). When you end the lesson, each student is emailed their own notes from your school Gmail. Nobody else sees them, you included. This needs a small Google Apps Script, which takes about 5 minutes to set up:

    
    
      
  1. Signed in as , open script.google.com → New project. Name it Live Lessons notes.
    2. 
      
  2. Delete what's in Code.gs, press Copy script below and paste it in. Press 💾 Save.
    3. 
      
  3. Press Deploy → New deployment → ⚙ Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone. Press Deploy, then Authorize access and allow it. If Google says the app isn't verified, press Advanced → Go to Live Lessons notes. It's your own script.
    4. 
      
  4. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec). Paste it into js/config.js as NOTES_EMAIL_URL, or send it to Claude to do it.
    5. 
    

    

    

    

    
The script only emails the signed-in student's own school address (it checks their Google sign-in), and at most 20 emails per student a day. If you ever change the school domain in config.js, copy and paste the script again, then use Deploy → Manage deployments → ✏ → New version.
